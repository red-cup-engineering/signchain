## Source-bound contract composition

The ordinary contract and instrument composition paths now retain their original
contract declarations as native SignCell evidence. A declared Hoare cut requires
an admitted schema boundary and an explicitly directed predicate implication
(or predicate identity). Two predicates sharing a consequence cannot license one
another. The existing SignChain rule owner derives the conditional composition
Cell from both source-bearing premise Cells; the complete rule and sources are
replayable through `admitContractCompositionEvidence`.

Missing evidence leaves the relevant Cell untold, with a separate pair-bound
diagnostic. These computations check the supplied declarations; runtime effects,
source authentication, global constraint support and customer acceptance remain
separate observations. Exposure constructors retain nested source data, and
duplicated time, observer and boundary coordinates must agree. See the
[source-bound contract specification](docs/source-bound-contract-cuts.md) for
the clauses, native carrier/JSON distinction and receiving conditions.

## Fixed telling histories

`TellingLog` from `@red-cup-engineering/signchain/log` receives ordered
positive and negative tellings during construction. It owns nested terms and
witnesses and indexes them by the original SignCell coincidence relation.
Call `new TellingLog(rows).seal()` before retaining or sharing a fixed source
observation. `seal()` returns that same log, irreversibly refuses later
`tell`/`deny` calls, and exposes the state as `sealed`. Rows, indexes and all
existing queries keep their original meanings. A later source observation
requires a new log; it cannot rewrite an earlier sealed history.

Logs remain appendable by default for existing builders and SEAVT replay.
`Object.freeze(log)` alone is not finalization: private storage needs the
native `seal()` guard. Finalization fixes the supplied history, not the truth
or authority of its testimony. It does not define L-Space; queries remain
operations over admitted observations of its points.

## Mark-rooted evidence, two orders and the identification frontier

SignChain consumes generated **SignCell** Mark/Utterance/predication cells.
It does not create the original marks, spatial geometry, lawfully attached
faces, or a free equivalence relation between two mark presentations.

The two evidence lanes are independently *told for* and *told against*.
The two order structures are the knowledge and truth readings over the
same generated FOUR carrier. Contradictory testimony must remain visible:
a `stable` knowledge charge records both evidence sides, rather than
collapsing to a Boolean true or false. Knowledge join/meet and truth
join/meet retain all original witness sources, even when their **value**
is no longer the directly measured evidence readout.

The original `bilattice.mjs` now exposes
`adjudicateCellEvidence(originalCell)`: a strictly finite, source-owned
judgment that separately reports the original *evidence reading*,
the written *value reading*, knowledge, truth, measurement agreement,
and one of `unobserved`, `supported-unopposed`,
`opposed-unopposed`, or `contested`. A supported observation is
only **eligible for a competent relation proof**. It is not such a
proof: `quotientAdmitted` remains false, including positive cases.
An explicit `Quot.sound` requires a proved equivalence relation;
one presented Mark coinciding with another as a byte string cannot
supply it. The current SignGraph `mark-gluing` conditional entrance
refuses opposed, contested, unmeasured or unobserved relations.

## Original-witness source withdrawal (research-facing finite operation)

The \`./source-withdrawal\` entrypoint reads a **sealed original SignChain
TellingLog**, an original unevidenced SignCell proposition, and an explicit
one-to-one mapping from its **actual source witnesses** to purported original
producer identities. It does not parse URLs into institutional identity.

\`assessOriginalSourceWithdrawal(history, {proposition,attributions})\`
computes the original single-cell knowledge/truth readings, then the
counterfactual readings after withholding all testimony from each supplied
producer in turn. Every result is derived by the existing SignCell cell
constructor and SignChain \`adjudicateCellEvidence\`, \`knowledgeLeq\` and
\`truthLeq\` over the original distinct evidence sides. This follows the
two-order FOUR source in Obsmetrologia's
\`Language/Positing/FiberAlgebra.lean\`; removing evidence cannot increase
the knowledge-evidence order, but can move in either direction in the truth
order. An independently verified source identity or equality proof is **not**
produced by the function.

A sociology negative control demonstrates two documentary witness rows with
distinct document identities but one asserted institutional producer: removing
that one producer changes the source-reported adoption proposition's
knowledge reading from \`potential\` to \`absurd\`. A contested public-intelligence
control begins at \`stable\` and yields \`active\` or \`potential\` when different
originals are withheld. Those are conditional finite evidence computations,
not evidence that the source was actually withdrawn, an institution adopted
a practice, or a scientific proposition is false. Neither attribution labels
nor source-reported claims are authenticated by the operation. New Work, rights
approval, proofs, publication and market settlement remain downstream.

Native receiving: \`node --test test/source-withdrawal.test.mjs\` against
installed original SignCell/Metrologic/FOUR dependencies; the package
\`receivingTests\` includes the ten positive and negative cases.


## Original evidence snapshot comparison (candidate, source-only)

The existing `./source-withdrawal` entrypoint now also exports
`compareOriginalEvidenceSnapshots(previous, current, {propositions})` for
research revision intake. Both inputs must be **sealed original SignChain
TellingLogs**. Callers explicitly nominate up to 128 **unevidenced original
SignCell propositions**; the operation does not try to discover all dependent
claims or guess source identities. It compares source witnesses on each
polarity using SignCell/SCA coincidence identity, returning separately:

- added and removed **original witness addresses** for each polarity;
- changed *observedAt* receipt-clock testimony attached to otherwise unchanged
  original witnesses, including absent vs explicitly present clock coordinates;
- the earlier and later knowledge/truth/posture readings;
- evidence-order directions in **both** original FOUR orders;
- `requiresReverification` whenever the *witness identities* change,
  even if the FOUR reading is unchanged;
- the addresses of affected nominated propositions for existing
  SignGraph/Work consumers to use in their **own** dependency rechecks.

An unchanged `potential` reading after replacing one paper with a different
paper is thus **not** mistakenly certified as unchanged evidence. Likewise,
a corrected reception timestamp forces re-verification even when the witness
identity and FOUR reading agree; neither timestamp is certified by this read. Repeated
tellings of the same source identity do not manufacture corroboration; opposing
testimony is not erased. Results are strictly conditional on supplied original
histories, bounded to 20,000 original indexed candidate-row visits across
both histories and all nominated propositions, and read-only. This is **not** a retraction, evidence authentication,
source-origin proof, graph traversal, task scheduler, active deployment, or
publication receipt. In particular, the caller must independently attest that
the snapshots represent real changes in a lawful chronology and that the
original source identities and rights are correct.


**Budget admission:** `TellingLog.boundedAt(proposition, maxCandidateReads)`
uses the existing original three-role index and checks the shortest bucket's
length before constructing any row array. The snapshot comparison shares a
20,000-candidate limit across all nominated propositions and both histories,
and rebuilds measured SignCells only from admitted rows. A large index bucket
may be refused even if most rows would fail another role predicate; this
conservative false refusal is necessary for the present bounded-reader
contract. The bound is on candidate-row visits and allocation, **not** the
total byte size of opaque witness payloads, cryptographic encoding effort, or
real-world scientific uncertainty. Native `where`/`against` remain
unbounded for other consumers.

Native proposed receiving:
`node --test test/source-withdrawal.test.mjs` with this repository's
authentic installed dependencies. Tests cover source replacement and changed reception clocks without
FOUR change, cross-polarity contradiction, explicit multi-proposition scope,
duplicate original tellings, empty/forged inputs, and all 16 finite
FOUR-polarity transitions. Merging and installed consumer receiving remain
separate from source-authored tests.
